# Applications

The Limits Before Scale principle can apply to multiple types of complex systems.

## Artificial Intelligence

AI systems require operational limits to maintain predictable behavior as capability and scale increase.

## Digital Platforms

Large platforms amplify both signal and noise. Structural limits are required to preserve system coherence.

## Organizations

Organizations that scale without structural constraints tend to accumulate complexity faster than control mechanisms.

## Financial Systems

Financial networks often experience instability when leverage and complexity grow faster than regulatory or structural limits.

## Infrastructure Networks

Large-scale infrastructure systems require operational boundaries to maintain reliability and stability.
