# Limit Types

Different systems require different types of limits to maintain stability when scaling.

## Cognitive Limits
Limits on decision complexity and information processing capacity.

## Informational Limits
Boundaries that preserve signal-to-noise ratio in large information systems.

## Organizational Limits
Structural constraints that maintain clarity of responsibility and decision authority.

## Computational Limits
Constraints on processing power, model capacity, and operational scope.

## Operational Limits
Boundaries that define what actions a system is allowed to perform.

When systems scale without clearly defined limits in these dimensions, instability tends to emerge.
