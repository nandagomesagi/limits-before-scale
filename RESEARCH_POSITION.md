# Research Position

## Status

**Limits Before Scale is a conceptual research framework, not a claimed scientific law.**

The repository distinguishes propositions, hypotheses, observations, and applied tests so that conceptual language is not mistaken for empirical certainty.

## Core proposition

> Scale amplifies the structure already present in a system.

The proposition directs attention to what a system contains before amplification: architecture, controls, constraints, dependencies, interfaces, and failure boundaries.

## Working hypothesis

> When complexity grows faster than effective limits, controls, and supporting architecture, instability and failure risk increase.

This is a research hypothesis to be examined across domains rather than treated as universally proven.

## AI research question

> What human, technical, operational, and governance architecture must exist before AI amplification is treated as sustainable?

## Analytical distinctions

This research separates:

- **conceptual proposition** — a structured claim intended for examination;
- **hypothesis** — a claim that can be tested or challenged through evidence;
- **observed evidence** — source-backed facts or measurements;
- **derived result** — an output produced from stated evidence and logic;
- **assumption** — an explicit scenario input that is not presented as observed fact;
- **unknown** — information that is not defensibly established.

## Falsifiability and revision

The framework should be revised when evidence contradicts its definitions, relationships, or claimed scope. Applied examples do not prove universal validity; they test whether the framework produces useful and defensible distinctions in a specific domain.

## Publication boundary

The public repository documents the conceptual research layer and selected applied evidence. Proprietary implementation mechanics, evaluation systems, datasets, workflows, and commercial logic remain outside the public research layer.

**Public thesis. Private mechanism.**
