# Individual Cognitive Architecture

Individual Cognitive Architecture is the functional map of how a person receives information, organizes language, rejects noise, makes decisions, executes actions, and interacts with AI systems.

It is not:

- therapy
- coaching
- religion
- personality typing
- generic productivity
- motivational content

It is a structural research layer for understanding how humans operate before AI amplification.

## Core Question

The question is not only:

> How should people use AI?

The deeper question is:

> What human architecture is being amplified?

## Operating Layers

This research observes patterns across:

- information input
- language structure
- noise sources
- decision patterns
- execution gaps
- preferred outputs
- blocked language
- human-AI interaction

## AI Operating Profile

The long-term output of this research is the concept of an AI Operating Profile: a structured layer that helps AI systems adapt to how a person operates.

This is not prompt engineering alone.

It is pre-prompt architecture.

## Position

AI does not replace cognition by default.

AI amplifies the architecture it finds.
